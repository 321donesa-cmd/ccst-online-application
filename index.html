Course Syllabi, Pricing & Schedule

SCT 10: Introduction to Concrete

Price: R4,500 ex VAT (R5,175.00 incl VAT) Date: 17-18 Feb | 14-15 Apr | 09-10 Jun | 11-12 Aug | 13-14 Oct Venue: Midrand / Cape Town / Durban

Recommended for SMMEs, junior technical and sales staff in building and construction, and anyone wanting a short introduction to concrete. Requires English literacy.

Full Syllabus
  • Properties of fresh and hardened concrete
  • Materials for concrete: cement, aggregates, water, and admixtures
  • Receiving, site handling, and storing materials
  • Batching, mixing, and standard quality testing
  • Transporting, placing, and compacting techniques
  • Finishing methods and surface preparation
  • Protection and curing principles
  • Formwork erection and reinforcement placement
  • Sand-cement mixes and mortar basics
  • Durability factors and environmental exposure

SCT 12: Mortars, Plasters, Screeds & Masonry

Price: R2,800 ex VAT (R3,220.00 incl VAT) Date: 19 Feb | 16 Apr | 11 Jun | 13 Aug | 15 Oct Venue: Midrand / Cape Town / Durban

Developed to assist NHBRC inspectors and contractors interpret the requirements of the NHBRC Home Builders Manual.

Full Syllabus
  • Factors that affect the strength and durability of concrete
  • Role and selection of cement, sand, stone, and water
  • Receiving, site storage, and stock control of materials
  • Mix proportions for concrete foundations and floor slabs
  • Floor screeds: preparation, bonding, laying, and curing
  • Slump test and cube test procedures and interpretation
  • Concrete and clay masonry: expansion and shrinkage characteristics
  • Cavity walls, block walls, and movement joint necessity
  • Properties of mortar and plaster; sand-cement mixes
  • Common causes of cracking in render and masonry and prevention

SCT 13: Making Concrete Bricks & Blocks

Price: R2,800 ex VAT (R3,220.00 incl VAT) Date: 20 Feb | 17 Apr | 12 Jun | 14 Aug | 16 Oct Venue: Midrand / Cape Town / Durban

Assists with empowering learners by providing a practical understanding of manufacturing quality masonry units.

Full Syllabus
  • Essential structural requirements of masonry units
  • Cement hydration principles and how to make masonry strong
  • Quantity of water necessary for optimal workability
  • Operation and maintenance of blockmaking machines
  • Selection of raw materials and optimum mix proportions
  • Curing protocols for newly manufactured blocks
  • Raw material storage and aggregate protection
  • Sand-cement mix ratios and moisture control
  • Compressive strength testing of masonry units
  • Building practices with masonry to minimise structural cracking

SCT 14: Concrete for RMC Truck Drivers

Price: R2,500 ex VAT (R2,875.00 incl VAT) Date: On Demand / Custom Request Venue: Midrand / On-Site

Provides Ready Mix Concrete (RMC) truck drivers with basic concrete technology and emphasizes customer representation.

Full Syllabus
  • Primary factors affecting concrete strength
  • Bleeding, workability, cohesion, and segregation phenomena
  • The slump test: step-by-step execution and practical meaning
  • The cube test: making sample cubes correctly on site
  • Hot and cold weather effects on concrete during transit
  • Delays, contamination, or spillage risks during transport
  • Site access evaluation and discharge safety
  • The detrimental effects of unauthorised water addition
  • Handling concrete transport around site after discharge
  • Customer relations, site etiquette, and truck washout procedures

SCT 15: Concrete for Batchers & Plant Staff

Price: R2,800 ex VAT (R3,220.00 incl VAT) Date: On Demand / Custom Request Venue: Midrand / On-Site

Essential concrete education and theory required for batchers and plant operators to produce quality ready mix concrete.

Full Syllabus
  • Responsibilities and duty of care of a concrete batcher
  • Constituent materials and their specific effects on concrete performance
  • Fresh concrete properties and mandatory compliance tests
  • Basics of mix design and total water content control
  • Testing protocols for hardened concrete properties
  • Client requirements, specifications, and customer satisfaction
  • Basic batch plant management, quality control, and assurance

SCT 20: Concrete Practice

Price: R9,800 ex VAT (R11,270.00 incl VAT) Date: 09-12 Mar | 18-21 May | 20-23 Jul | 14-17 Sep | 16-19 Nov Venue: Midrand / Cape Town / Durban (4 CPD Points)

Recommended for foremen, clerks-of-work, technicians, and supervisors. Minimum entrance: Grade 10 and basic arithmetic.

Full Syllabus
  • Properties of fresh, early age, and hardened concrete
  • Materials for concrete: cements, aggregates, admixtures
  • Mix proportions, yield calculations, and quantities
  • Concrete production, batching plant, and site mixing
  • Transporting, placing, compacting, protection, and curing
  • Formwork design principles, strike times, and reinforcement detailing
  • Joint types, construction joints, and sealing practices
  • Sand-cement mixes, low-density concrete, and prestressed/precast units
  • Concreting in hot and cold weather; surface defects and repairs
  • Off-shutter architectural finishes, sampling, and compliance testing

SCT 21: Concrete Industrial Floors on Ground

Price: R3,200 ex VAT (R3,680.00 incl VAT) Date: 24 Mar | 26 May | 28 Jul | 22 Sep Venue: Midrand / Online (1 CPD Point)

Gives engineers and contractors a detailed and practical overview of industrial floor design and construction. Entrance: Grade 12.

Full Syllabus
  • Concrete pavement design philosophy, subgrades, and subbases
  • Damp-proofing membranes and sub-slab friction control
  • Joint design: construction, contraction, isolation joints, and dowel placement
  • Concrete production, placing, and side form setup
  • Advanced construction methods: vacuum dewatering, edging, and laser screeding
  • Finishing techniques, joint sawing schedules, and joint sealants
  • Surface treatments, dry-shake hardeners, toppings, and curing protocols
  • Adverse weather precautions and special floor applications

SCT 22: Concrete Road Design & Construction

Price: R3,500 ex VAT (R4,025.00 incl VAT) Date: 25 Mar | 27 May | 29 Jul | 23 Sep Venue: Midrand / SARF Administered (1 CPD Point)

Focuses on technology applied in rigid pavement design and construction using cncPave software. Degree/Diploma recommended.

Full Syllabus
  • Rigid pavement types, structural behavior, and failure modes
  • Subgrade, subbase, and subsurface drainage design
  • Mechanistic thickness design using computer program cncPave
  • Concrete materials, mix design, and flexural strength target setting
  • Joint load transfer, joint layout, sealants, and reinforcement detailing
  • Mechanised paving (slipform) vs. labour-intensive road construction
  • Inlays, overlays, low-volume concrete roads, and rehabilitation techniques

SCT 30: Concrete Technology

Price: R12,500 ex VAT (R14,375.00 incl VAT) Date: 02-06 Mar | 11-15 May | 13-17 Jul | 07-11 Sep | 09-13 Nov Venue: Midrand / Cape Town (5 CPD Points)

Intensive course for engineers and technologists. Recommended for mining/electrical/mechanical engineers. Entrance: Grade 12 (Maths/Science) + 2 yrs experience.

Full Syllabus
  • In-depth properties of fresh, early age, and hardened concrete
  • Cementitious materials, extenders (fly ash, slag, silica fume), and aggregate chemistry
  • Mixing water quality and chemical admixture mechanisms
  • Comprehensive concrete mix design calculations
  • Concrete production, placing, compaction, formwork pressures, and curing
  • Reinforcement, joint design, defects, blemishes, and repair methods
  • Specialised mix designs, sand-cement mixes, and architectural concrete
  • Temperature effects and thermal cracking controls in mass concrete

SCT 37: Durability of Concrete

Price: R3,200 ex VAT (R3,680.00 incl VAT) Date: 26 Mar | 28 May | 30 Jul | 24 Sep Venue: Midrand / Online (1 CPD Point)

Overview of concrete durability for engineers, technologists, and foremen involved in specifying or placing durable concrete.

Full Syllabus
  • How cement hydrates and works to ensure low permeability
  • Mechanisms of mechanical deterioration (abrasion, erosion, cavitation)
  • Mechanisms of chemical deterioration (carbonation, chloride ingress, sulphate attack, ASR)
  • Practical methods of making concrete impermeable and resilient
  • Durability testing methods: oxygen permeability, sorptivity, and chloride conductivity

SCT 41 & 42: ICT Stage 2 & 3 (General & Practical)

Price: R14,000 per stage ex VAT (R16,100.00 incl VAT) Date: Self-Study | Revision: April 2026 | Exams: May & Oct 2026 Venue: E-Learning (Exams in Midrand)

Bridging courses offered by the Institute of Concrete Technology (London). Prerequisite for ACT 50 Diploma.

Full Syllabus
  • SCT 41 (Stage 2): General Principles of Concrete Technology (Part 1)
  • SCT 42 (Stage 3): Practical Applications of Concrete Technology (Part 2)
  • Includes 36 self-study lessons covering all ICT London requirements
  • Specialist lecturer supervision, guidance, and input via email
  • Compulsory online revision workshops prior to examinations
  • Preparation using compilation of ICT past examination papers

ACT 50: Advanced Concrete Technology

Price: R16,500 per module ex VAT (R18,975.00 incl VAT) Date: Year-Round Open Registration Venue: Modular Self-Study (ICT Stage 4)

Leading worldwide qualification in concrete technology examined by ICT London. Set text: Fulton’s 10th Edition. Requires pass in SCT 41 & 42.

Full Syllabus
  • Module 1: Materials and Mixes (Cementitious chemistry, aggregates, admixtures)
  • Module 2: Concrete Properties (Microstructure, rheology, mechanical performance)
  • Module 3: Concrete Manufacture, Site Practices, Quality Control & NDT
  • Module 4: Concrete Sustainability, Environmental Stewardship & Statistics
  • Module 5: Advanced Concrete Technology Individual Project
