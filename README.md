Within the CCST Education Programme 2026, the General Information and Policies establish the administrative, financial, academic, and operational framework for all training courses
. As CCST transitions into a modernized technology-enabled institution, these guidelines ensure standardized delivery across foundational modules, professional courses, and international certifications
.
1. Registration Framework
Application Procedure: Prospective delegates must submit a completed official registration form (downloadable from www.ccst.org.za or found within the program guide) directly to the SCT Manager via email
.
Mandatory Details: Registration forms require full personal information, a copy of an ID document, educational/tertiary qualifications, company invoicing details, and authorization from the employer or sponsoring party
.
Cut-Off Dates: For e-learning modules under the Institute of Concrete Technology (ICT Stage 2 & 3 / SCT 41 & 42), registrations are accepted until 15 January 2027
.
2. Course Fees, Invoicing & Payment Terms
Payment Terms: All course fees are payable in advance
. Bookings are only confirmed upon receipt of full payment or an official Purchase Order (PO) from registered vendors
.
Invoicing & Quotations: Quotations are issued upon receiving a completed registration form
. Invoices must be paid within 30 days of receipt
.
Certificate Issuance: Course completion certificates are strictly withheld until full proof of payment has been receipted by the SCT Manager
.
Banking Details: Direct deposits or Electronic Fund Transfers (EFT) are made to the Cement and Concrete School of Technology NPC account at First National Bank (FNB) Midrand (Account No: 63107309999, Branch Code: 210835, SWIFT: FIRNZAJJ)
. Proof of payment must specify the learner’s name, course, and quotation/invoice reference number
.
Incremental Payment Option: For the Advanced Concrete Technology Diploma (ACT 50), a modularized self-study structure allows delegates to pay per module, avoiding a single large upfront cost
.
3. Cancellations & Postponement Policy
Delegate Cancellations: Cancellations received less than 5 business days prior to the course start date, as well as non-attendance, attract a 100% cancellation charge
.
ICT Exam Cancellations: For ICT Stage 2 and Stage 3 examinations (SCT 41 & 42), cancellations must be submitted before the end of February 2027 to avoid incurring exam registration fees
.
Cancellation/Postponement by CCST: CCST reserves the right to postpone or cancel scheduled courses at any time due to unforeseen circumstances or insufficient demand
. Confirmed registrations will receive a full refund or a credit toward a future class
.
4. Academic, Examination & Rewrite Guidelines
Entrance Prerequisites: Learners must satisfy specified entry requirements per course (e.g., English literacy and basic arithmetic for SCT 10/20
; Grade 12 with Math & Science + 2 years experience for SCT 30
). Successful completion of both ICT Stage 2 (SCT 41) and Stage 3 (SCT 42) is a strict, non-negotiable prerequisite for ACT 50
.
Examination Timing & Location: All official ICT examinations (Stage 2, Stage 3, and ACT) take place from 11:00 to 14:00 exclusively at the Midrand training centre
.
Exam Scripts & Remarking: Examination queries, scripts, and remarking requests for ICT-accredited courses are governed directly by the Institute of Concrete Technology (ICT) in London
.
Test Rewrites: Rewriting a course test may be permitted depending on the delegate's initial results and requires mutual written agreement with the SCT Manager
.
5. Safety, Meals & Student Welfare
Laboratory Safety: Candidates attending classroom-based modules in the CCST training laboratory must wear safety shoes and avoid loose-fitting clothing
.
Meals & Catering: Tea, coffee, and lunch are included in classroom course fees
. Special dietary requirements must be arranged 72 hours in advance with the SCT Manager and may carry an additional charge
.
Travel & Accommodation: Learners and their employers are fully responsible for arranging and funding all travel and accommodation logistics
